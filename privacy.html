Institutional Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Hadi Education System, Balkasar, Chakwal

1. Information We Collect

When parents, guardians, or prospective students interact with our online portal or submit an admission inquiry, we collect only the necessary details required to evaluate applications and communicate effectively:

  • Parent / Guardian Name: For personalized communication.
  • Student Name & Academic Grade: To determine appropriate class placement (Classes 6–12, Hifz, or Matric/College tracks).
  • Telephone / WhatsApp Number: For interview scheduling and administrative confirmation.
  • Email Address: For written correspondence and application status notifications.
  • Inquiry Notes: Specific questions regarding boarding, curriculum, or transportation.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information submitted through our forms is utilized strictly by the Hadi Education System Admissions Directorate to:

  • Contact parents regarding entrance interviews and document verification.
  • Provide timely answers to campus visit and curriculum questions.
  • Maintain institutional records required for Federal Board (FBISE) registration.

3. Strict No-Sale & Third-Party Protection

We uphold the highest ethical standards in student and family privacy. We never sell, lease, trade, or disclose your personal contact details to external marketing agencies or third parties. Information is processed exclusively by our internal administrative staff and reputable notification service providers (such as transactional email dispatch).

4. Cookieless Browsing & Session Privacy

Our website is intentionally built to minimize data collection:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not place third-party advertising or profiling cookies on your device.
  • Cookieless Analytics: Any aggregated page performance analytics are privacy-first, cookieless, and do not track users across domains.
  • Temporary Session State: Ephemeral browser storage (sessionStorage) is used solely to prevent repeating decorative page intro animations on consecutive visits within the same browsing session.

5. Data Retention & Family Rights

Inquiry records are retained securely for a period of up to 12 months to assist families throughout the annual admissions cycle. Parents may contact our administrative office at any time to verify, update, or request the deletion of their contact information from our active communication databases.
